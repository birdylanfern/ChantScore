## Chant data

- Psalm tone melodies: Source & Summit psalm-tone data (supplied by the project owner).
- Vulgate psalm & canticle texts (`src/lib/tones/vulgatePsalmsData.ts`): public-domain (The Unlicense) liturgical Vulgate psalms and canticles from Benjamin Bloomfield's `jgabc` project (https://github.com/bbloomf/jgabc).
- Reading tone formulas: described in the CMAA "Sung readings in the Ordinary Form" tutorial, after the Liber Usualis.
